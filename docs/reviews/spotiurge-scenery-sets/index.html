Spotiurge: Unsplash scenery sets matched to covers

Native macOS arm64 captures from --demo-shot. Before: origin/main (b70d35b). After: this branch. Same demo data, page, and logical window size (normal 1440x900, narrow 900x760). Demo scenery is World Scenery, the photo of the day for October 8, 2026.

What changes: Home and Settings show the set's photo of the day in place of the bundled lake. Album, artist and playlist pages each show the photo closest to their cover's colours. The black-and-white album gets a grey lake, the misty teal artist photo gets a misty lake, and the golden sunset playlist gets an orange canyon. The dark wash is slightly heavier (0.612 to 0.63), so dim text keeps 4.5:1 over any photo. The Scenery picker and photo credit are in Settings → Appearance, below the first screen of Settings, so these captures do not show them.
