Spotiurge for iPhone: developmental interface candidate

Native SwiftUI candidate in apps/ios, captured on the iOS Simulator (iPhone 18 Pro, iOS 27.0). Provenance and hashes: interface-captures.json. No physical iPhone was used. Demo captures use synthetic data and say so on screen.

What this shows

  • The desktop control-room language on a phone: Inter ranked by weight, the cover-lit room (amber key light and teal counter-light when nothing plays), system Liquid Glass only on controls, content on the room.
  • One amber lamp for live state: primary play discs, the current tab, the playing row's lamp bar, EQ glyph and lamp-text title, a selected Love, and switches (DESIGN.md's recorded exceptions). Choices use the neutral inverted pill; errors and cautions stay neutral.
  • Opaque overlays: the taste editor and Discovery options sheets sit on the overlay colour; Now Playing on the room. The For you options are a sheet, not a translucent menu.
  • Honest states: playback reports "Not signed in" or "unavailable" and never controls the Spotify app; unmatched picks are counted and collapsed; an unpaired phone says so.
  • Accessibility sizes stack the exploration channels instead of truncating them, and Now Playing scrolls.

No baseline

There is no earlier Spotiurge iPhone interface. The playback probe's laboratory screen shows different content and state, so it is not offered as a before image. Before is disabled rather than faked.

Not shown or not verified

  • Signed-in Spotify library, search and catalogue matching against real data; a paired private-cloud sync; a real GPT-6 Luna answer.
  • Audio: the linked development engine uses the stock librespot identity that Spotify refuses on iOS.
  • Motion smoothness, the 220 ms fader glide and 600 ms room cross-fade in motion, and live Reduce Motion changes. The Reduce Motion captures are static.
  • Any physical-iPhone run of this app, VoiceOver walkthrough, iPad, landscape.
